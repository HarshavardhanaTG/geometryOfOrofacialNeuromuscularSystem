# EMG based speech neuro-prostheses

We are developing EMG based speech neuro-prostheses, and the paper titled, [Geometry of orofacial neuromuscular signals: speech articulation decoding using surface electromyography](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.02591) is out first work towards its development.

Here, we conduct a series of experiments to demonstrate the viability of EMG-to-language translation. We explain the graph structure of the orofacial neuromuscular signals and build efficient models to learn spatio-temporal EMG graph. 

We explain the inherent geometry of the EMG signals and the signal distribution shift across individuals. 

We collect data from `16` individual subjects and open source the data. [Download our data](https://osf.io/ym5jd/). The data is about `40 GB`.
